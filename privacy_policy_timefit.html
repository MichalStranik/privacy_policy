Privacy Policy for TimeFit

Effective Date: May 20, 2024 • Last Updated: May 20, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to TimeFit ("we", "our", or "us"). TimeFit is a smart task planning and calendar scheduling application designed to help users organize their daily schedules, manage recurring tasks, resolve calendar conflicts, and receive timely task notifications.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the TimeFit mobile application (the "App").

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

TimeFit operates primarily on a local-first model. The following data is processed and stored locally on your device:

  • Tasks and Schedule Data: Title, timing, duration, recurring task rules, completion status, and user settings (e.g., planning horizon settings).
  • Calendar Data: When you grant calendar permissions, TimeFit accesses your device's native calendar to:
    • Read existing events (READ_CALENDAR) to detect scheduling conflicts.
    • Write app task occurrences (WRITE_CALENDAR) to your selected calendar.

B. Device Permissions

To provide its core task scheduling and notification capabilities, TimeFit requests the following device permissions:

  • Calendar Access (READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR): Required to synchronize your tasks with your personal calendar and automatically detect external calendar conflicts.
  • Notifications & Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Required to send start-time task alerts, deliver missed task reschedule prompts, and re-arm exact alarms after a device reboot or app update.
  • Audio & Camera (RECORD_AUDIO, CAMERA): Requested only if you use audio notes, voice input, or photo attachments within the app. Audio and photo data remain on your device unless explicitly shared by you.

C. Automatically Collected Usage and Diagnostic Data

To ensure app stability, improve performance, and fix bugs, TimeFit integrates third-party diagnostic tools provided by Google:

  • Google Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous usage statistics (such as feature interactions, screen views, and device model/OS version).
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymized crash logs, stack traces, and system performance metrics when an error or crash occurs.

These analytics services do not collect your personal task names, descriptions, or calendar contents.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or task details to third parties or advertisers.

We only share anonymized diagnostic and analytics data with trusted service providers strictly necessary for operating and improving the App:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase Services: For crash reporting and anonymous usage analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy for more details.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in the good-faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal obligations or protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Data Security and Retention

  • Local Storage: Your task data and app configurations are stored securely in your device’s local database sandbox (Room / DataStore), protected by Android's security architecture.
  • Retention: Your task data remains on your device for as long as you choose to keep the App installed. You can delete individual tasks or clear all app data at any time through Android Settings.

5. Your Choices and Controls

You have full control over your data and device permissions:

  • Calendar & System Permissions: You can grant or revoke permissions (Calendar, Notifications, Camera, Microphone) at any time via Android Settings > Apps > TimeFit > Permissions.
  • Data Deletion: Deleting tasks inside the App removes them from the app database. Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored app data.

6. Children's Privacy

TimeFit is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
